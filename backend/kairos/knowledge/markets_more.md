# Market mechanics: circuit breakers, expiries, calendars

## Market-wide circuit breakers in India
tags: circuit breaker market wide india nse bse 10 15 20 percent halt sebi
Under SEBI's 2013 rules, a 10%, 15% or 20% move in the Nifty 50 or the Sensex (whichever comes first, either direction) halts all equity trading. 10%: a 45-minute halt before 1 pm, 15 minutes between 1 and 2:30 pm, none after 2:30 pm. 15%: 1 hour 45 minutes before 1 pm, 45 minutes between 1 and 2 pm, and the rest of the day after 2 pm. 20%: trading stops for the day. Trading resumes with a 15-minute pre-open call auction. The 10% breaker was hit on 13 and 23 March 2020.

## Market-wide circuit breakers in the US
tags: circuit breaker us s&p 500 level 1 2 3 7 13 20 percent luld halt
US exchanges halt all stocks when the S&P 500 falls 7% (level 1) or 13% (level 2) from the previous close before 3:25 pm New York time (a 15-minute halt), and for the rest of the day at 20% (level 3). They were introduced after the 1987 crash (the Dow fell 22.6% on Black Monday) and were triggered four times in March 2020. Single stocks have "limit up–limit down" bands that pause trading for five minutes after sharp moves.

## Indian market calendar and mechanics
tags: india expiry f&o ban t+1 pre open budget results season fii dii muhurat
NSE equity trades 09:15–15:30 IST after a pre-open session (09:00–09:15). Settlement is T+1. Since September 2025 NSE's index and stock derivatives expire on Tuesdays (weekly Nifty, monthly on the last Tuesday). Stocks whose open interest passes 95% of the market-wide limit go into the F&O ban (no new positions). Foreign (FII/FPI) and domestic (DII) institutional flows are published daily and move large caps. Key dates: the Union Budget (1 February), RBI policy (six times a year), results seasons (mid-January, April, July, October), and Diwali's Muhurat session.

## US market calendar and mechanics
tags: us calendar fomc cpi jobs report earnings season opex triple witching russell rebalance
Regular hours 09:30–16:00 New York time (19:00–01:30 IST in winter, 20:00–02:30 in summer). Market movers: FOMC decisions (eight a year, 14:00 New York), CPI (mid-month), the jobs report (first Friday), earnings seasons (from mid-January, April, July, October, led by the big banks and then the giant tech companies), monthly options expiry (third Friday; quarterly "triple witching" in March, June, September, December) and the Russell index reconstitution in late June.
