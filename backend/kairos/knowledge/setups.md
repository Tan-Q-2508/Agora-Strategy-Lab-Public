# Setups: the events scanners look for

## Breakaway gaps
tags: breakaway_gap_up breakaway_gap_down gap breakout volume earnings gap
A gap that clears the previous candle's whole range and closes beyond the last 20 candles' range, on at least 1.5× average volume. These often start new moves (earnings and big news cause most of them) and tend not to fill quickly. Kairos's stop for the setup is the edge of the gap: if it fills, the idea is wrong.

## Island tops and bottoms
tags: island_top island_bottom island reversal stranded gap
A gap up, one to ten candles, then a gap down (or the mirror) that strands those candles on an island. Everyone who bought on the island is losing at once. Bulkowski found islands frequent but unreliable on their own (high failure rates), so Kairos weighs them by what they did on your market.

## NR7 and inside-bar breakouts
tags: nr7_up nr7_down inside_break_up inside_break_down narrow range toby crabel volatility contraction
Toby Crabel's NR7: the narrowest range of the last seven candles signals coiled volatility; the next close beyond its high or low often starts an expansion. An inside bar (a candle inside the previous one) is the same idea; the breakout of the mother candle is the trade. Contraction precedes expansion: the direction comes from the break.

## Springs and upthrusts (false breaks)
tags: spring upthrust wyckoff turtle soup false breakout failed breakout stop run liquidity sweep
Richard Wyckoff's spring: the price dips below an established low (shaking out weak holders and triggering their stops), then closes back above it. The upthrust is the mirror at highs. Larry Connors' "turtle soup" is the same trade against the Turtles' 20-day breakout rule, requiring the old extreme to be at least four days old. Smart-money traders call it a liquidity sweep. The stop is just beyond the false break's extreme.

## Key reversal days
tags: key_reversal_up key_reversal_down key reversal outside reversal one day reversal
A new 10-candle low followed by a strong close above the previous close (bullish), or a new high and a weak close below (bearish). It shows a one-day change of control; more meaningful after a long move and on high volume.

## Pocket pivots
tags: pocket_pivot pocket pivot morales kacher volume institutional buying
Gil Morales and Chris Kacher's pocket pivot: an up day closing above the 10-day average on more volume than any down day of the last ten. It is meant to spot institutions buying inside a base, before a classic breakout.

## New 52-week highs and lows
tags: new_high_52w new_low_52w 52 week high momentum anchoring george hwang
George and Hwang (2004) showed that stocks near their 52-week high keep outperforming: investors anchor to the old high and under-react to good news. Kairos flags the first close above the past year's high in a month; new 52-week lows are the bearish mirror (weak stocks tend to stay weak).

## Golden and death crosses
tags: golden_cross death_cross 50 200 day moving average cross long term trend
The 50-day average crossing above (golden) or below (death) the 200-day. Widely watched by media and funds, slow and often late: many crosses come after half the move. As a regime filter (only buy above, only short below) it has kept trend followers out of the worst bear markets.

## Squeeze breakouts
tags: squeeze_up squeeze_down squeeze breakout ttm bollinger keltner volatility
When Bollinger Bands sit inside the Keltner Channel volatility is unusually low; the first close outside the bands after a squeeze often starts a strong move. Carter's rule is to trade in the direction of the momentum line; Kairos flags the close outside the band.
