# Trading psychology and process

## Common mistakes
tags: psychology mistakes fear greed fomo loss aversion revenge overconfidence
Cutting winners early and holding losers (loss aversion), chasing moves after missing them (fear of missing out), trading bigger after losses to win it back (revenge trading), and trusting a few lucky wins (overconfidence). Rules decided before the trade, written stops and fixed risk per trade protect against all of them.

## A professional process
tags: process plan journal review checklist discipline
Before each trade: what is the setup, where is it wrong (stop), where is it right (target), how much is at risk, and does it fit the plan? After each trade: record the reason, the result in R, and what you'd do differently. Review weekly: which setups made money and which didn't. Kairos keeps exactly this journal for its desk and learns from it.

## Probabilistic thinking
tags: probability uncertainty edge sample size luck
Any single trade is mostly luck; an edge shows only over many trades. Judge decisions by whether they followed a sound process, not by one outcome. Think in ranges, not certainties: that's why Kairos gives price ranges with a measured track record instead of a single predicted price.
